Prove it with your own Concordium wallet

Before Kuasa insures an agent, its owner proves they are 18 or older and live in the EU, with a zero-knowledge proof: Kuasa learns those two facts and nothing else. This page runs that step with your wallet, on testnet.

  1. Kuasa anchors a Verify & Access V1 request on-chain, bound to your account.
  2. Your wallet shows the two statements; you approve or reject them.
  3. Kuasa checks the proof against the chain and anchors the audit record.

Needs the Concordium Wallet on Testnet with an identity (the Concordium testnet identity provider issues one in a minute). No CCD needed: Kuasa pays the anchors.
