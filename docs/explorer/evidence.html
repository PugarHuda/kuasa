kuasa-market v3: no bond escape, no fake covers

Security review follow-up. A cover contract is bound to its insurer at registration, must run the kuasa-cover module (checked on-chain by module reference) and must name the registering account as its pool; slashes go into a 7-day escrow the 2-of-3 juror panel can veto, so an insurer approving fake claims to its own accounts cannot get its bond back faster than by leaving. Both insurers registered (passing those checks) and left v2. Claim 2 of the staged test was also slashed on v2 before the move
