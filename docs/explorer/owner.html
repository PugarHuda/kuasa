Own an agent, with your own wallet

Register an AI agent, prove who stands behind it, give it a spending mandate, insure it and take it back. You sign every step in the Concordium Wallet; Kuasa pays the fees, so you need no CCD.

Needs the Concordium Wallet on Testnet with an identity. Rather watch first? One minute with a real wallet.

  1. Connect your wallet

    Kuasa sees your account address, nothing else.

  2. Register your agent

    Mints your agent in Concordium's Agent Registry, owned by you.

  3. Prove who stands behind it

    18 or older and living in the EU, as a zero-knowledge proof bound to this agent.

  4. Give it a mandate

    50 EUDemo over 30 days. Shops and insurers check it before every payment.

  5. Get test EUDemo

    Concordium's EUDemo allowlist asks your wallet to prove EU nationality, then sends test euros to your account.

  6. Insure your agent

    Pay 0.5 EUDemo for 10 EUDemo of cover over 7 days. You receive a cover certificate (a CIS-2 token) your wallet can show.

  7. Take it back

    Revoke the mandate. From the next block, shops refuse the agent.
