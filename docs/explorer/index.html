When your AI agent pays twice, its insurance pays you back.

Kuasa Cover insures what AI agents do with real money. You prove who you are once, your agent buys cover for what it spends, and when it gets something wrong the claim is paid from evidence on the chain, in minutes.

Agents spend on their own now. They also get it wrong.

Payment guards try to stop a bad payment before it happens. Some still get through, and today nobody carries that loss. Kuasa does.

It pays twice for the same order.

The insurer watches every finalized block. It finds the duplicate, pauses the agent at every shop and files the claim for you.

It buys outside what you allowed.

Your rules are signed and on-chain. A purchase that breaks them is refused at the shop, and one that slips through is a valid claim.

It harms a merchant.

The shop claims on the agent's cover directly, so merchants can trust agents they have never met.

If the insurer does not pay, its bond does

Every insurer posts a CCD bond on an open registry. If it approves a claim and leaves it unpaid, anyone can flag it. After a grace period anyone can slash the bond by the size of the claim, and after a short window for the juror panel to veto, the claimant is paid by the contract.

No arbiter has to agree, and the insurer cannot leave early to dodge it.
